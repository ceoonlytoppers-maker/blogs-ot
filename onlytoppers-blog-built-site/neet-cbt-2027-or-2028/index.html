NEET CBT Mode: 2027 or 2028? Latest Update & How to Prep

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Your feed is probably full of "NEET is going online" reels. Some say 2027. Some say 2028. Some say "hybrid". Let's sort the noise from the facts in about 5 minutes, then get you a prep plan that works either way.

TL;DR

  • NEET UG moving to a computer-based test (CBT) has been announced by the Centre and NTA.
  • The start year is not confirmed. Fresh reports say 2027 may stay pen-and-paper (or run as a hybrid), with full CBT likely from 2028.
  • The Nandan Nilekani-led exam reform task force has had its tenure extended to 27 January 2027. The final call is expected after its report.
  • Syllabus, shifts, marking and normalisation details are not officially announced.
  • Smart move: prep for both formats. Your concepts matter far more than the mode.

Wait, so is NEET 2027 CBT or not?

Here's the timeline in plain words.

After the NEET UG 2026 paper leak controversy, the Centre announced that NEET would move from OMR to computer-based testing. NTA later told the Supreme Court the same thing, and reports talked about a multi-day exam across around 1,000 centres.

Then, in early November 2026, newer reports said the plan may be slower than expected:

  • Officials reportedly feel big changes are hard to roll out in time for a May 2027 exam.
  • Some southern states, including Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Karnataka, have raised concerns about infrastructure and access for rural students.
  • A hybrid setup (some pen-and-paper, some CBT) has been reported as an option for 2027, with the full shift in the 2028-29 cycle.

Important: these are reports citing sources, not an official NTA notification. Until NTA publishes the bulletin, treat everything as "likely", not "locked".

Will CBT make NEET harder?

No. Changing the mode doesn't change how tough a Physics numerical or a Biology question is. Your concepts decide your score.

What can feel different at first:

  • Reading long questions on a screen
  • Moving between questions without flipping pages
  • Tracking time with an on-screen timer
  • Doing calculations on rough sheets while looking at a monitor

That's a practice gap, not a difficulty jump. You can close it in a few weeks.

Shifts and normalisation: what to know

If a CBT exam runs over several days, different papers may need a fair way to compare scores. That's what normalisation is for. NTA has not announced any NEET normalisation formula, so anyone selling a "normalisation calculator" right now is guessing. Don't waste study hours on it.

How to prep for both formats (the lock-in plan)

1. Keep paper practice as your base. If 2027 stays OMR, you still need speed and accuracy with a pen. Keep solving PYQs and chapter tests on paper.

2. Add one weekly CBT test. Take a timed chapter test on a laptop or desktop. Phones don't count. Build up to full-length CBT mocks as the exam gets closer.

3. Practise the skip-flag-return habit. Don't sink 4 minutes into one question. Move on, mark it, and come back.

4. Use rough sheets smartly. Physics, Chemistry and diagrams still need paper. Get comfortable switching between screen and sheet.

5. Review every mock like a detective. Tag each lost mark: concept gap, silly mistake, or time management. This is where scores really go up.

6. Protect your eyes and sleep. Take screen breaks. No all-nighters before mock tests.

Does the syllabus change?

The shift to CBT is about the exam mode, not about throwing away what you've studied. Keep revising NCERT, keep solving PYQs, and adjust only if NTA's official notification says otherwise.

FAQs

Will NEET 2027 be conducted in CBT mode?

It's not confirmed. CBT has been announced as the direction, but recent reports say 2027 may remain pen-and-paper or hybrid, with full CBT likely from 2028. Wait for NTA's official bulletin.

Is NEET CBT tougher than pen and paper?

No. The mode changes how you attempt the paper, not how hard the questions are.

Will the NEET syllabus change because of CBT?

No syllabus change has been announced because of CBT. Follow NTA's official notification.

Will there be normalisation in NEET?

Not announced. If the exam runs in multiple shifts, NTA will publish the method.

How should I practise right now?

Keep paper practice as your base and add weekly screen-based chapter tests, then full-length CBT mocks.

When will the final format be announced?

The reform task force's report is expected by 27 January 2027, and NTA's bulletin should follow. We'll update this post when it does.
